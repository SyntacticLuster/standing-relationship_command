# Standing™ Product Vision

## Positioning

Standing is not an AI CRM.

Standing is a **temporal relationship system of record**.

Traditional CRMs are organized around records, pipelines, stages, and manual data
entry. Standing is organized around the changing state of human relationships.

The durable object is the person.

Threads, emails, meetings, notes, commitments, signals, organizations, drafts,
and inferred state are observations around that person over time.

## Core Question

Standing should help answer:

> Where do I stand with this person, what changed, and what needs to happen next?

## Four Primary User Questions

1. **Who matters?**
2. **What changed?**
3. **What do I owe them, or what do they owe me?**
4. **What should I do next?**

## Product Thesis

Communication is not the product.

Communication is the evidence stream.

The product is the changing state of the relationship.

Standing should ingest evidence from communication and activity, transform it
into structured relationship state, retain provenance, track changes over time,
and surface the next meaningful action.

## Long-Term Value

After sustained use, Standing should know:

- who matters
- how the user knows them
- how communication patterns changed
- what each person cares about
- what was promised
- what happened afterward
- what cadence is normal
- which signals predicted outcomes
- what the user tends to forget
- what requires attention now

That accumulated, provenance-backed relationship memory is the long-term moat.

## Product Wedge

> Standing remembers the state of your relationships and tells you what matters next.
