# Standing™ Feature Ideas

## Standing Summary

A concise current-state object for each relationship.

## Relationship Timeline

Preserve meaningful state transitions over time so Standing can answer:

> How did this relationship get here?

## Relationship Diff

Show only what changed since the user's last meaningful interaction.

## Why Now?

Explain why a person or thread is being surfaced.

## Open-Loop Resolution

When later correspondence appears to satisfy an earlier commitment:

> This appears to resolve "Send execution ledger." Close loop?

## Cadence Normalization

Learn each relationship's normal interaction rhythm and detect meaningful
deviations.

## Pre-Meeting Brief

Before a meeting:

```text
Meeting with Nora in 18 minutes.

Last touch:
Technical diligence email

Open loops:
2

Overdue:
1

Changed since last conversation:
- investment intent increased
- architecture evidence requested
```

## Post-Meeting Capture

Capture new facts, commitments, state changes, follow-ups, and momentum.

## Compose Preflight

Before send, check unanswered questions, missing promised attachments,
unresolved commitments, contradictions, stale assumptions, tone consistency,
and unsupported assertions.

## Relationship-Aware Search

Examples:

- Who am I waiting on?
- Who is waiting on me?
- Who has gone cold?
- Who needs a follow-up before Friday?
- Which relationships have increasing momentum?
- Who mentioned a specific topic?
- Who should I contact today?

## Source Inspection

For extracted facts or signals, expose:

- Why?
- Source
- Confirm
- Correct
- Forget

## Relationship Graph

Eventually support introduction paths and opportunity-context questions.

## Memory Aging

Allow facts to become confirmed, stale, superseded, disputed, or forgotten.

## Personal Attention Model

Standing can learn what the user tends to miss, such as delayed replies,
forgotten attachments, unresolved promises, neglected warm relationships, or
over-attention to low-value threads.

## Features to Delay

Do not prioritize yet:

- autonomous sending
- team CRM workflows
- sales stages
- elaborate dashboards
- LinkedIn scraping
- giant chatbot UI
- graph database migration
- broad provider matrix
- configurable automation builders
- marketing automation
- enterprise org-chart features
