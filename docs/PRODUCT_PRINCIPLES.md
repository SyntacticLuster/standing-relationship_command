# Standing™ Product Principles

## 1. Relationship State Over Contact Records

The person is the durable object. Communication is evidence.

## 2. Provenance Before Confidence

Observed facts, user notes, derived signals, and model inference must remain
distinguishable.

## 3. Identity Owns Data

Workspace ownership must derive from authenticated server-side identity.

## 4. Open Loops Are More Important Than Generic Tasks

Standing should understand who owes whom what.

## 5. Explain Prioritization

Whenever Standing ranks someone highly, explain why.

## 6. Learn Normal Cadence

A daily relationship and a quarterly relationship should not share the same
cooling threshold.

## 7. Preserve the Delta

Users often need what changed, not the entire history.

## 8. Compose With Context, Not Just Generation

Check for forgotten attachments, unanswered questions, broken commitments,
tone discontinuity, unsupported claims, and stale assumptions.

## 9. Memory Should Age

Facts can become stale and require confirmation, supersession, dispute, or
forgetting.

## 10. Automation Should Reduce Bookkeeping

If users must constantly curate Standing like a CRM, the product has failed.

## 11. Private Means Private

Authentication, tenant isolation, minimization, correction, deletion, export,
and provenance are product requirements.

## 12. Avoid Feature Gravity

Do not let Standing become a generic CRM, sales automation suite, giant chatbot,
or analytics warehouse.

Every feature should answer:

> Does this help the user understand relationship state or act correctly on it?
